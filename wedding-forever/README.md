# Wedding Forever

Wedding Forever is a static story experience made with HTML5, CSS3, Vanilla JavaScript, and original SVG artwork. It runs directly from `index.html`; there is no package manager, framework, server, or build step in this folder.

## Pages

- `index.html` — story introduction and clickable 13 chapter timeline
- `ponnu-paarkum.html` — first meeting
- `family-acceptance.html` — family acceptance
- `engagement.html` — engagement
- `preparations.html` — wedding preparations
- `invitation.html` — invitation
- `celebrations.html` — celebrations
- `vratham.html` — vratham and blessings
- `reception.html` — reception
- `wedding.html` — wedding day
- `post-wedding.html` — first morning
- `first-journey.html` — first journey
- `forever.html` — the years after

The shared design and interactions live in `wedding-forever.css` and `wedding-forever.js`. Every chapter has its own illustration and two keepsake images in `assets/`.

The countdown date is editable and remembered in the visitor’s browser. Gallery artwork opens in a lightbox; sharing uses the device share menu where available, with a copy-link fallback. WhatsApp links contact Vedakkodi at 8531046561.

To add this sample to the Vedakkodi repo, upload the `wedding-forever/` folder and replace the root `anniversary-surprise-website.html` and `sitemap.xml` with the included updated copies.
